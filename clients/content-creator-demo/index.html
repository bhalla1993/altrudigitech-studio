Filming since 2019

The story

Hi, I'm Jordan. Pull up a chair.

I started filming makeup tutorials in a rented bedroom with one lamp and a phone propped on a stack of textbooks. Seven years later the lamp is a ring light, the textbooks are a tripod, and the part I still love most is the comment that says, “I tried it and it worked on my skin.”

My content lives in two worlds: polished, step-by-step beauty on one channel, slower and cozier lifestyle on the other. Brands come for the tutorials and stay for the trust that comes from both.

Ways to work together

Four formats, one clear brief.

Pick the shape that fits your launch. Every format includes concept approval, one revision round, and a short wrap-up report.

Integrated mention

Your product woven naturally into a tutorial or GRWM my audience already watches, with a clear spoken and on-screen disclosure.

  • 60 to 90 second segment inside a long-form video
  • Pinned link and description placement
  • Cut-down clip for Reels and TikTok included
Best for: launches & shade extensionsTurnaround: ~3 weeks
Brief this format

Dedicated video

A full video built around one product or collection: first impressions, wear test, and an honest verdict.

  • 8 to 12 minute YouTube feature with custom thumbnail
  • Two short-form cutdowns for Instagram and TikTok
  • Optional 30-day whitelisting for paid amplification
Best for: hero productsTurnaround: ~4 weeks
Brief this format

UGC package

Authentic, creator-style clips delivered to you to run on your own channels and ads. No posting required on my side.

  • 3 to 5 vertical videos, hooks and CTAs tailored to your brief
  • Raw footage and captions delivered via shared folder
  • Usage window agreed upfront, extendable
Best for: paid social teamsTurnaround: ~10 days
Brief this format

Ambassador season

A three-to-six month partnership with recurring content, so your product becomes part of the story, not a one-off.

  • Monthly integration across YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok
  • Seasonal moments: holidays, back-to-routine, gift guides
  • Quarterly check-in with performance snapshot
Best for: brand-buildingTerm: 3 to 6 months
