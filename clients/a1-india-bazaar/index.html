Welcome to A1 India Bazaar

Right on Fairburn Street in Greater Sudbury, A1 India Bazaar is the neighbourhood spot for produce, grocery, dairy, and Indian sweets — the same mix they put on their sign and social posts every week.

Walk the aisles for basmati, lentils, masalas, chai, frozen Vadilal favourites, namkeen, bakery items, and celebration mithai. They’re open seven days a week with hours that work for after-work runs and weekend stocking up.

Call (705) 669-1451 before you visit if you’re hunting a specific brand or checking weekly specials.
